Privacy Policy

How the IMS handles employee account information, inventory records and related system activity.

1. Scope of this notice

This notice describes how the Hospital Warehouse IMS application handles information entered by authorized warehouse personnel. It applies to the website, employee accounts, inventory records and administrative tools.

2. Information we store

The system may hold employee usernames, names, contact information, job titles and profile details. It also stores inventory items, quantities, prices, batches, expiry dates, document references, department destinations, timestamps and activity or audit logs.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate the warehouse, control access, document receipts and issues, calculate stock reports, investigate errors and support system recovery. Warehouse records are not intended as personal patient medical records.

4. Authorized access

The application uses account permissions. Staff access is controlled by administrators, and editing is restricted by warehouse branch and role. Certain backup and restoration actions are reserved for higher-privilege administrators.

5. Hosting and backups

Application data is stored on the configured server and database storage. Authorized administrators may export backups. If Google Drive backup integration is enabled, encrypted copies may be stored in the connected account. Backup scheduling and cloud storage depend on configuration.

6. Session and security information

The system uses login sessions and security-related information to authenticate users and protect access. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security; administrators should protect credentials and review access permissions.

7. Retention and corrections

Inventory and audit records may be retained to support stock reconciliation and accountability. Corrections should follow approved warehouse procedures. Retention, deletion and access requests must be handled according to the hospital’s adopted rules and applicable law.

8. Responsibilities and enquiries

Employees should enter accurate data, keep their credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access. Questions about records or account information should be directed to the hospital’s designated system administrator.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the IMS features, hosting arrangements or the hospital’s internal policies change. The responsible institution should review and approve the final text before official use.
