Privacy Policy

A general overview of how information may be handled when using a warehouse inventory management application.

About this application

For users with authorized application accounts.

Back to home

1. Information recorded

The application may store account details such as names, contact information and access roles, as well as product, batch, transaction and inventory records entered by authorized users.

2. How information is used

Recorded information supports inventory tracking, stock reconciliation, expiry monitoring, authorization checks, activity history, and generating reports.

3. Access and permissions

Access to stored records depends on the permissions configured by the application administrator. Users should access only the information needed for their assigned responsibilities.

4. Data storage

Application information is stored in the database and infrastructure selected for deployment. Storage arrangements and access policies depend on the particular installation.

5. Backups

Where configured, database copies may be downloaded by authorized administrators or stored as encrypted backups using an external storage provider. Backup access and retention should be managed securely.

6. Logs and security

The application may record sign-ins, edits, inventory movements, and corrections to support accountability and troubleshooting. Access controls and audit records help protect records but cannot eliminate every security risk.

7. Retention and changes

Retention periods, account information changes, and deletion requests depend on the way the application is operated and the laws and policies applicable to the deployment.

8. Questions

For questions about data entered into a particular installation, contact the administrator responsible for that deployment.
