Terms of Use

The rules and responsibilities for using the central hospital warehouse application.

Draft — subject to institutional review
Warehouse IMS documents

For authorized staff and administrators.

Back to home

1. Authorized use

The IMS is intended for personnel authorized by the hospital to manage its central warehouse. Public account creation is not available. Access must be granted and managed by designated administrators.

2. Accurate warehouse records

Users must enter complete and accurate information for items, batches, manufacturing and expiry dates, quantities, prices, departments, paper references and stock movements. The system is a recordkeeping tool and does not replace physical stock verification.

3. Account responsibilities

Each employee must use their own assigned account, keep their password private and avoid sharing access. Administrative or branch permissions must not be bypassed.

4. Stock movements and corrections

Receiving, distribution, transfers, returns and opening balances must correspond to actual warehouse operations. Users must follow the hospital’s authorization process, including special procedures for controlled medicines. Corrections and reversals should remain traceable.

5. Expiry and stock recommendations

Expiry warnings and FEFO suggestions assist staff in selecting usable stock, but users remain responsible for checking the physical product, correct batch, storage conditions and applicable clinical or regulatory rules before distribution.

6. Backups and availability

The application may offer manual database backups and optional configured cloud backups. Users should not assume a backup has completed unless its status is confirmed. Technical availability and recovery depend on hosting, storage, maintenance and properly tested restore procedures.

7. Monitoring and audit logs

The application records activity such as sign-ins, stock movements and administrative changes to support accountability and investigation. Users must not alter records or perform actions outside their permissions.

8. Administration and policy updates

The hospital or designated operator determines approved procedures, account access and acceptable use. Terms may be revised as the application evolves; the responsible institution should review and approve the final terms before official use.

Need clarification?

Contact your hospital’s designated warehouse system administrator.
