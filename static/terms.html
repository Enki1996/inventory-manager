Terms of Use

General usage guidelines for an inventory tracking application.

About this application

For users with authorized application accounts.

Back to home

1. Purpose

The application is a tool for recording warehouse inventory, batches, expiry dates, receipts, issues, transfers, returns, and associated documents.

2. Account access

Accounts are provided and managed by authorized administrators. Users must not share credentials or attempt to bypass assigned permissions.

3. Accuracy of records

Users should record accurate quantities, dates, prices, and document references. Application records do not replace verification of physical inventory.

4. Inventory movements

Movements and corrections should represent authorized actions. Undo and Redo create additional traceable records and are not substitutes for documenting an actual physical return.

5. Recommendations

Expiry alerts and FEFO suggestions are decision aids. Users must independently verify the physical item, stock lot, condition, and any applicable procedures before distribution.

6. Availability and backups

Application availability depends on the hosting environment. Backups and restoration should be configured, monitored, and tested independently. No individual backup is guaranteed until it is verified.

7. Acceptable use

Users should not falsify records, misuse accounts, access information without authorization, or interfere with normal operation of the system.

8. Updates

Features and these general usage guidelines may change as the application evolves. Applicable obligations depend on the individual deployment and relevant law.

Using this application

These are general usage guidelines and do not identify a particular organization or create a guarantee of service.
